What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Turn requirements into a garment brief

Explain the need an existing line cannot meet. Include visual references, working conditions, colors, desired materials and functional details.

Agree sample approvals

Define how fit, measurements, materials and branding will be reviewed. Sample quantities, costs and adjustments must be agreed in the proposal.

Produce against an approved reference

Document model, sizes, quantities, materials and applications before authorization. Discuss retained references for future replacements.

Before you choose

Consider custom development for a specific design or function; compare existing garments if they can meet the requirement.
