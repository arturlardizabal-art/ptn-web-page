What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Start with the work environment

Describe tasks, working conditions, frequency of use and company requirements. These help assess fit, fabric and identification needs. A heavy-duty appearance alone is not a technical specification.

Build a size and allocation matrix

List pieces per employee, area and size. Separate initial allocations from replacements and identify the delivery site for each group.

Keep identification consistent

Review logos and department identifiers with the garment material. Recurring uniform programs and multi-location distribution can support ongoing requirements.

Before you choose

Send the exact technical specification for safety-related requirements. Photos do not establish flame, chemical, electrical or certified high-visibility protection.

Do you offer gabardine or denim workwear?

Not automatically. Request product specifications and evidence for any specialized protection your operation needs.
