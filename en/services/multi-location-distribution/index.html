Uniform distribution to branches across Mexico

PTN offers multi-location distribution of uniforms and textile supplies in Mexico. Its published scope includes consolidating orders, separating goods by location and shipping to branches, plants or retail sites through DHL and FedEx. Confirm quantities, destinations, costs and timing per project.

What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Keep a location-level breakdown

List each branch's garments, sizes and quantities. Distinguish the total project from each site's allocation.

Provide complete receiving details

Include addresses, postal codes, receiving contacts and delivery restrictions. Identify any receiving windows for logistics review.

Agree tracking responsibilities

Define shipment information and who follows up. Update garment references and destination details before repeat orders.

Before you choose

Compare the complete supply, separation and transport cost for the same destinations. Do not assume simultaneous delivery or shipping included in garment prices.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help plan your order

Can uniforms go to several cities?

Yes. PTN publishes nationwide multi-location distribution; send quantities and destinations for review.

Which carriers are mentioned?

PTN lists DHL and FedEx. Confirm the carrier and specific coverage in your proposal.

Is shipping included in garment prices?

Confirm all supply, separation and transport charges in the quote.

Will every branch receive goods on the same day?

No simultaneous delivery guarantee is stated. Agree timing based on preparation, destinations and transport conditions.
