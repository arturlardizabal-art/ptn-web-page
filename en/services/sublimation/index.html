Textile sublimation for business apparel in Monterrey

PTN offers sublimation as a customization technique for compatible polyester garments. Composition, base color and artwork must be reviewed first. Apparel labeled as sportswear or polyester does not automatically produce the same result across models.

What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Start with composition

Provide the fabric specification. Blends and finishes can affect the process; ask for a suitable garment base if none has been selected.

Review base color and artwork

Specify the textile color, application areas and design colors. Screen artwork does not show how the same design will appear on every fabric.

Agree the scope of application

Define a logo placement or larger coverage project. The published textile service does not automatically include every promotional item or all-over apparel design.

Before you choose

If the garment is unsuitable for sublimation, compare DTF, screen printing or embroidery.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help plan your order

Can a cotton tee be sublimated?

Do not assume compatibility. PTN's published offer refers to polyester garments; ask for material review and alternatives.

Is every polyester garment compatible?

Fiber content, base color and finish need review for the specific model.

Should I use sublimation or DTF?

Select the garment first, then assess compatible processes.
