What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Coordinate teams without identical cuts

Different roles can use complementary shirts and blouses within the same visual identity. Review colors and logo placement across the full set.

Review sizing by model

Collect required sizes and use the chosen model's size chart. Agree how measurements, samples and garment approval will be handled before ordering.

Choose existing lines or custom development

Published lines offer defined models. If a specific fit or design is needed, discuss made-to-measure development, including samples, quantities and timing.

Before you choose

Compare the same model, composition and branding. Confirm replenishment conditions to maintain a consistent team image.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help plan your order

Can you coordinate women's and men's lines?

PTN's published offer includes both. Request compatible models and colors for your team.

Where should the logo go?

Send the artwork, desired dimensions and placement. The fabric and design determine a suitable application.

Can you develop an exclusive design?

PTN offers made-to-measure design and manufacturing. Samples, minimums and timing depend on the project.

How do we order for new hires?

Keep model, color, size and customization references. A uniform program can support repeat requirements.
