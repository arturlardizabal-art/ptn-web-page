Recurring uniform supply programs for businesses

A PTN uniform program addresses recurring supply, replacements and scheduled deliveries. It starts with agreed garment references and a size and quantity matrix. Inventory arrangements, frequency, minimums and lead times must be explicitly defined in the proposal.

What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Create an internal garment catalog

Record models, colors and branding by role so new hires and replacements use clear references.

Agree frequency and quantities

Share staff levels, estimated consumption and peak requirements. A program does not automatically mean unlimited stock or instant deliveries.

Coordinate sites and owners

Define who consolidates orders and receives goods at each location. Multi-branch distribution can be discussed within the operating plan.

Before you choose

Consider a program for recurring replacements, new hires or multiple teams. Compare continuity terms and operational responsibilities alongside cost.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help plan your order

How does a program differ from a single order?

It establishes references and recurring replenishment arrangements rather than addressing only one requirement.

Does it include permanent reserved stock?

Not automatically. Reserved inventory and availability terms must be agreed with PTN.

Can several uniform lines be included?

You can propose industrial, executive and casual lines with quantities by role.

How do we handle new hires?

Use agreed garment references and sizes, and confirm ordering notice and availability requirements.
