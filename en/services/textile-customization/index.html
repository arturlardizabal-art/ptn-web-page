What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Embroidery adds thread texture

Embroidery creates a raised design. Letter size, design density and the supporting fabric affect suitability, so review the artwork before selecting the finish.

Printing depends on artwork and volume

Screen printing suits repeated designs; DTF is a transfer option for multicolor artwork. Sublimation requires a compatible composition and base color.

Record a clear specification

Identify technique, dimensions, placements, colors and garment in the quote. Retain approved artwork and specifications for future replacements.

Before you choose

Compare options on the same garment and design. Quantity and placements affect cost; no technique is always the cheapest or best.

What to send for a quote

A useful quote starts with clear information

  • Artwork and approximate dimensions
  • Garment, composition and color
  • Front, back or sleeve placements
  • Total quantity and design variations

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help plan your order

Should we embroider or print?

Embroidery adds thread texture; printing supports different detail and surface requirements. Evaluate the fabric and desired finish.

Should we choose DTF or sublimation?

Garment composition and color are key. Send both with your design for a compatibility review.

Which artwork file should I send?

Send the best available original, ideally a vector file, and approximate dimensions. PTN confirms production requirements.

Can I supply my own garments?

Confirm this arrangement directly with PTN, including garment composition, quantity and condition.
