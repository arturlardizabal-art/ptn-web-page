What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Use comparable specifications

Explain the garment or product you will make. Share a technical sheet or ask how a reference sample can be reviewed.

State quantities and units

Specify meters, rolls or another clear unit. Confirm sales format and minimums. Different fabric widths can change usefulness for the same project.

Plan color and repeat supply

For staged production, identify recurrence and color references. Confirm availability and lot conditions for each delivery.

Before you choose

Compare composition, width, expected use and logistics alongside the purchase price, rather than price per meter alone.
