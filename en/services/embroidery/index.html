What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Review real-size legibility

Small letters and fine lines may require adjustment for thread. Specify dimensions and assess how the logo will read at its actual size.

Consider the fabric

A shirt, polo and work shirt may need different treatment. Review placement, dimensions and thread colors with the garment material.

Keep teams consistent

Retain approved artwork and colors for repeat orders. Include personal names or individual identifiers as variations from the start.

Before you choose

Choose embroidery when thread texture suits the identity and use. Compare printing for gradients or very fine artwork.
