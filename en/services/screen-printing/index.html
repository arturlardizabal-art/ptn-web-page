What to consider for this service

Define the scope and purchasing decisions

Group runs by design

List quantities for each design and garment color. Different departmental artwork creates variants that a total piece count alone does not describe.

Define colors and placements

Front, back and sleeves are separate locations. Include dimensions, color references and placement details with the selected fabric.

Compare processes where needed

For individual names or other design requirements, compare DTF or embroidery. Assess the complete project and intended finish.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help plan your order

Is screen printing suitable for large runs?

It is worth evaluating for repeated artwork. Cost suitability depends on colors, placements, garments and quantity.

Can you print front and back?

Include both locations and dimensions for PTN to review the full application.

What if each garment has a different name?

Identify all variations. Consider a different technique for individual elements.

How does it compare with DTF?

They are different application processes. Compare the same artwork, fabric, placements, quantity and desired finish.
